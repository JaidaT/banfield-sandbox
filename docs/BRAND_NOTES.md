## Services **[fit] [questions]**

Architectural Design; Interior Design; Renovation Design; Kitchen and Bathroom Design; Permit Coordination; Furniture and Soft Goods Design; Generational Design (aging in place, multi-generational); Passive House and Net Zero Design; and:

## Who is a good fit **[fit]**

**Strongest alignment** (Banfield's own words): renovation design; kitchen, bath and full-home redesigns in older homes; townhouse, duplex and condo reworks for end users; multi-generational and aging-in-place design; Passive House / Net Zero custom homes and deep renovations.

**Weaker fit:** highly transactional, entry-level condo buyers; investor-heavy presale condo markets; purely trend-led, low-involvement clients looking for fast decoration rather than planning and process.

**Ideal clients** are Lower Mainland homeowners going through a life transition (growing family, relocating, downsizing, rethinking how the home works). Often dual-income professionals, creatives, consultants and business owners. They want clarity and leadership, prefer fewer and better decisions, and mostly arrive by referral or word of mouth.

**Their challenges before hiring Banfield:** overwhelmed by decisions and short on time; worried about budget creep and hidden costs; unsure how to turn ideas into a buildable plan.

## Voice and language **[client] [questions]**

- **Personality:** approachable professionalism, calm confidence, thoughtful intelligence, warm hospitality, practical creativity.
- **Write as if speaking to a client in a meeting:** clear and concise, conversational but professional, confident without exaggeration.
- **Preferred words:** thoughtful design, real life, functional flow, tailored spaces, seamless process, designed with intention. Emotional words: home, gather, grow, morning, table, stay, belong, remember, hold, everyday, together, comfort.
- **Avoid:** overusing "luxury"; trend-based language; technical terms without context; leading with certainty or transactional language ("guaranteed", "risk-free"). **Warmth leads, proof follows.**
- **Ready-made CTA line:** "Grab a coffee. Let's talk about your home. Book a complimentary Meet + Inspire session to explore your space, goals, and next steps."
